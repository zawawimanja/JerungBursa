FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is "Smart Money" or "Jerung" in Bursa Malaysia context?
"Jerung" (Malay for shark) refers to large institutional investors — such as EPF, PNB, KWAP, Khazanah, and licensed fund managers — whose buying and selling activity creates significant volume footprints in the market. JerungBursa tracks these footprints using turnover thresholds (≥RM 2.0M–2.5M daily) and floor structure analysis.
What is the "Fresh Rider" strategy?
Fresh Rider focuses exclusively on recently listed IPO stocks (2024–2026) in their early accumulation phase. Because these stocks lack long-term overhead resistance, breakouts from well-built support bases tend to be cleaner and faster. The strategy enters on Day 1 of a qualifying setup with a maximum 5% stop loss below the support floor.
How does the "3 Black Swan Risk Check" protect me?
Before any signal is recommended, JerungBursa automatically screens for 3 event risks that historically caused the biggest losses: (1) Ex-Dividend Trap — entering within 10 days of an ex-date causes automatic price adjustment; (2) QR Earnings Binary Risk — entering 3-5 days before quarterly results is a coin-flip event; (3) Post-IPO Debut Dump — buying in the first week of listing when IPO allottees are offloading.
Is JerungBursa a financial advisor or investment service?
No. JerungBursa is a quantitative research and educational analytics platform. All data, signals, scores, and charts are for research and educational purposes only. We are not licensed financial advisors. Always conduct your own due diligence (DYOR) and consult a certified financial planner before making any investment decisions.
How often is the data updated?
Market data is updated automatically after Bursa Malaysia closes at 5:00 PM. Final settlement data from Yahoo Finance typically stabilizes between 5:50 PM – 6:05 PM. The dashboard reflects the latest available data with a timestamp shown on each update.
What is the recommended capital per trade?
JerungBursa simulates returns based on RM 2,000 per trade entry with a maximum of 2–3 concurrent positions. This portfolio split ensures maximum risk per day is ≤ RM 100 (5% of RM 2,000), protecting overall capital even if multiple trades hit stop loss simultaneously.
