Jerung Bursa Quantitative Methodology & Design

An in-depth study on how our algorithms scan the Bursa Malaysia stock market, compute support floors, and formulate disciplined VVIP Trend Rider investment models.

01. Introduction to Bursa Malaysia's IPO Landscape

The initial public offering (IPO) market in Bursa Malaysia — particularly within the ACE Market and Main Market — represents one of the most dynamic and high-growth segments of the Malaysian equity landscape. In recent years, interest in newly listed enterprises has surged significantly, driven by active retail participation and strong domestic institutional backing.

However, investing in newly listed IPO equities presents unique structural challenges. Because these companies lack long-term historical price charts on the exchange, conventional technical analysis indicators (such as long-term moving averages or classic oscillators) frequently fail or generate misleading signals. Many market participants suffer significant drawdowns by chasing high-momentum spikes at local tops or failing to detect quiet institutional accumulation zones.

JerungBursa was built as a quantitative research engine to bridge this information asymmetry. We believe that beneath seemingly random price movements lies the footprint of institutional Smart Money ("Jerung"), which can be systematically identified by combining volume spread dynamics, price support floor structures, and disciplined statistical modeling.

02. The Hybrid "Trend Rider" Strategy & Support Floor Model

At the core of the JerungBursa dashboard lies our flagship hybrid model known as VVIP Trend Rider (Method C). This framework seamlessly combines trend-following momentum investment principles with strict risk parameters derived from empirical Support Floor levels.

"The primary goal of the Trend Rider strategy is not to predict exact price peaks, but to ride institutional uptrends supported by Smart Money volume as long as price holds firmly above empirical support bases."

Below are the critical components of our Trend Rider quantitative model:

  • Support Floor: The lowest price boundary within a daily consolidation structure where institutional buying activity is densest. We compute this floor dynamically based on daily low interactions and cumulative volume concentration.
  • Floor Proximity Volatility Filter: We prioritize counters trading in close proximity to their Support Floor (e.g., within ≤ 6% for fresh IPOs, and ≤ 11% for mature equities). This ensures an optimal Risk-to-Reward ratio at position entry.
  • Disciplined Stop Loss (SL 3%): Rather than applying arbitrary percentage stops, Stop Loss levels are placed mathematically at 3% below the Support Floor. A breach of this level signifies a breakdown in the accumulation structure, triggering an immediate exit to preserve capital.
  • Riding Trends Without Rigid Profit Caps: Equities breaking out from institutional bases possess substantial upside momentum. The Trend Rider model avoids rigid Take Profit targets, allowing gains to compound dynamically as long as trailing trendlines remain intact.

03. Data Screening Mechanics & Volume Filtering

Dashboard metrics update systematically via automated data collection scripts (`scrape-real.js`). Our quantitative screening pipeline operates in two distinct phases to ensure maximum data integrity:

Phase 1: Baseline Market Data Audit. Algorithms filter out "Comb Stocks" — illiquid equities characterized by thin trading volume and flat, tick-by-tick price charts. Trading in illiquid counters exposes investors to severe liquidity risk, making position exits difficult during market downturns.

Phase 2: Smart Score Evaluation. Counters passing baseline audits receive a quantitative Smart Score of up to 15 points evaluated across the following parameters:

  1. Distance to daily Support Floor (Maximum 4 points).
  2. Number of support floor retests without breakdown (Touch Count - Maximum 3 points).
  3. Price consolidation tightness (Maximum 2 points).
  4. Price positioning relative to SMA 50 & SMA 200 moving averages (Maximum 3 points).
  5. Daily liquidity depth and average market Turnover (Maximum 3 points).

04. Risk Management & Portfolio Simulation Boundaries

We emphasize that technical models on JerungBursa are designed strictly to eliminate emotional human bias during trading execution. Tools like the Trading Base Calculator enable investors to calculate precise lot sizing based on total equity capital and individual risk tolerance before entering trades.

Under our backtested portfolio allocation framework, capital is divided across a maximum of 5 concurrent positions (Portfolio Split). For instance, with a RM 10,000 trading capital, maximum exposure per VVIP swing trade is capped at RM 2,000. This systematic diversification protects the broader portfolio if any single trade hits its 3% Stop Loss trigger.
