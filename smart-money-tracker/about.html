About Jerung Bursa

Bursa Malaysia Smart Money & Volume Analysis System

Our Mission

At JerungBursa, our mission is to democratize quantitative stock market analytics for retail investors. We design algorithms rooted in mathematical modeling and Volume Spread Analysis (VSA) to track institutional capital flows (Smart Money or "Jerung") across Bursa Malaysia, with a specialized focus on newly listed IPO equities.

What We Do

We provide a live market screening console that categorizes equities into disciplined quantitative models:

📈 Trend Rider Strategy

Identifies equities consolidating near tight support floors to systematically ride institutional uptrends.

🌱 Early Spring (Wyckoff Spring)

Detects post-correction accumulation bases reclaiming key first-day listing opening prices.

🎣 Bottom Fishing

Identifies deep value setups exhibiting early institutional re-accumulation at structural support levels.

Background & Expertise

JerungBursa was founded by quantitative market enthusiasts and experienced Bursa Malaysia retail investors. We strongly advocate that discipline, risk management, planned position sizing, and objective data analysis are the cornerstones of long-term trading success.

Please note that JerungBursa does not provide personalized investment advice. All tools and indicators are designed strictly for educational and historical backtesting purposes.
