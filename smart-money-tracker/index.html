📊 Istilah Lajur Data (Terminology)

Support Floor: The nearest floor support price (Floor Low) detected via historical data clustering. It represents the Smart Money defense line.
Distance to Floor: Jurang peratusan (%) di antara harga semasa dengan Support Floor. Had maksimum entry zon manis yang disyorkan adalah $\le 5.0\%$.
Volatility (Low Tight): Price fluctuation width rate. Lower percentages (e.g. under 5.5%) indicate tight price coiling (Close Tightness), signaling silent accumulation by institutional whales.
Number of Touches: Berapa kali lilin harga harian telah menyentuh dan disokong oleh Support Floor. Semakin banyak sentuhan (Touches $\ge 3x$), semakin kebal dan kuat lantai pertahanan tersebut.
Smart Score: Skor automatik berasaskan merit (maksimum 15 markah) yang menilai kualiti IPO, sentuhan lantai, volatiliti sempit, dan isyarat rejection. Skor $\ge 11$ dikategorikan sebagai sangat baik.
