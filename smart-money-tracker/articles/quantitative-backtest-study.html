1. 🔥 NEW (Day 1) Breakouts: The Purest Asymmetry

Day 1 signals represent equities breaking out from their primary foundation (Base 1). Because these stocks have not yet extended, they offer the tightest risk-to-reward ratio in the market.

  • Lowest Initial Risk: Initial Stop Loss can be anchored directly below the primary base floor (typically 3.0% to 4.5% below entry).
  • Zero Overhead Supply: Particularly in fresh IPOs, lack of historical trapped bagholders allows explosive acceleration once volume ignites.
  • Real Market Examples: PENTECH on September 08 (clean base lift-off), SUM on September 11.

2. ⭐ ADD-ON A+ (Early) & 🛡️ ADD-ON (Tight Floor): The Power of Confirmation

Contrary to retail intuition that "buying higher is riskier", the quantitative data revealed that ADD-ON A+ pullbacks exhibited the highest Win Rate (82.9%) and the highest Profit Factor (13.39x) of any category tested. Why?

💡 Institutional Confirmation: An ADD-ON candidate has already demonstrated that institutional smart money is actively supporting the trend. By waiting for a staircase consolidation (Base 2) where the stock builds a fresh, tight floor (≤ 3.5% distance) backed by multi-million ringgit turnover (≥ RM 2.0M – RM 2.5M), traders enter with proven institutional sponsorship.

Prime Add-On Case Studies

  • SUNLOGY: Base 1 staircase (+17% from original base), Turnover RM 2.8M – 3.1M, floor at RM 0.470 just 2.1% away, pristine uptrend aligned above SMA50/SMA200.
  • EIPOWER: Base 2 staircase (+18% to +35% from origin), Turnover RM 2.5M, Tightness 3.16%, floor tightly respected at RM 0.790 / 0.770 (1.9% – 3.2% risk).
  • STRATUS: Base 2 tight floor with RM 6.2M massive turnover, ultra-compressed tightness 1.08%, new floor at RM 2.680.

3. The Death Trap: ⚠️ ADD-ON Pucuk & Stale Runners

The backtest also unequivocally proved where retail traders consistently lose money: chasing overextended stocks without support floors, and buying stale "lemau" tickers.

Empirical Warning: Stocks extended more than 10% to 15% above their nearest technical floor (e.g., entering KEEMING when the floor is 14% away) or buying low-turnover, aged runners (> 60–100 days old with sub-RM 1.5M turnover like AMS) produced a catastrophic 7.1% Win Rate and a miserable 0.18x Profit Factor. Without an immediate floor to anchor risk, a normal market pullback triggers an unrecoverable 10%–15% drawdown.

The "1 NEW + 1 ADD-ON" Portfolio Pairing Strategy

Based on these quantitative findings, JerungBursa established the official 1 NEW + 1 ADD-ON allocation SOP:

  1. Slot 1: One 🔥 NEW (Day 1) Candidate — Provides maximum explosive upside potential with ultra-tight primary risk.
  2. Slot 2: One ⭐ ADD-ON A+ or 🛡️ Tight Floor Candidate — Provides rock-solid stability and high-probability continuation (82.9% Win Rate).
  3. Slot Discipline: Total active holdings strictly capped at 2 to 3 counters simultaneously to eliminate capital dilution and emotional overtrading.

Multi-Step Scale-Out Profit Locking Formula

To capture multi-week trend runners without prematurely forfeiting gains, execution adheres to the 3-phase scale-out formula:

Phase 1 (+5% Gain): 🛡️ Free Trade Lock — Move Stop Loss from support floor to entry price (breakeven). The position is now completely risk-free.

Phase 2 (+10% Gain / TP1): 💰 Bank 50% Cash — Liquidate half (50%) of total lots. Lock guaranteed profits into the account.

Phase 3 (+20%+ Gain / TP2): 🚀 Ride the Institutional Wave — Elevate trailing stop on the remaining 50% to TP1 (+10%), allowing the remainder to run to full multi-bagger potential without downside risk.
